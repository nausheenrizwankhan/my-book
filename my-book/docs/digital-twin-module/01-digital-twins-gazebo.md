# 1. Digital Twins and Physics Simulation with Gazebo

This chapter introduces the concept of digital twins for humanoid robots and explores how Gazebo facilitates physics simulation.

## What are Digital Twins?

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical object or system. In robotics, a digital twin of a humanoid robot allows us to simulate its behavior in a virtual environment, test control algorithms, and analyze performance without needing to interact with the physical robot.

## Physics Simulation with Gazebo

Gazebo is a powerful 3D robot simulator that accurately and efficiently simulates robots in complex indoor and outdoor environments. It provides robust physics engines (like ODE, Bullet, Simbody, DART) that handle gravity, collisions, and other physical interactions.

### Gravity and Collisions

Gazebo's physics engine allows us to define the gravitational forces acting on the robot and its environment. It also detects and resolves collisions between robot parts and between the robot and its surroundings, providing realistic feedback for testing and development.
